Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy outlines how DarkLore Automation (developed and operated by UdemyTAG Studio) handles authentication, data access, and API interactions when connecting with Google Cloud and YouTube API Services.

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 Last Updated: November 9, 2026 Developer: UdemyTAG Studio

1 Application Overview & Purpose

DarkLore Automation is an internal, automated multimedia production and publishing suite created and operated by UdemyTAG Studio. The application is designed to render scripted horror, mystery, and historical documentary video content ("DarkLore"), generate corresponding titles, descriptions, and tags, and programmatically publish these videos to the channel owner's authorized YouTube channel.

We are firmly committed to user privacy, cryptographic token hygiene, and compliance with the Developer Policies established by Google LLC and YouTube.

2 Google OAuth & YouTube API Services Compliance

Mandatory YouTube & Google Policy Reference

DarkLore Automation utilizes YouTube API Services to conduct video uploading and channel management workflows. By using, authorizing, or integrating with DarkLore Automation, you explicitly acknowledge and agree to be bound by the:

  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Google Privacy Policy

DarkLore Automation strictly abides by the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3 Information We Access and Scopes Requested

DarkLore Automation requests only the minimum necessary Google OAuth 2.0 permission scopes required to perform video publishing tasks on behalf of the authenticated channel owner:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload: Permits the application to upload video files directly to the authenticated YouTube channel, assign user-defined video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, category, privacy status), and set thumbnail assets.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly (or basic channel identifier): Used exclusively to verify the channel identity and ensure uploads are directed to the correct destination channel.

What we DO NOT access: DarkLore Automation does not access, read, or process personal emails, contacts, search history, watch history, private playlists of other users, payment details, or personal profile data.

4 Local Storage, Encryption & Zero Third-Party Sharing

Strict Local Token Security Guarantee

All OAuth 2.0 access tokens, refresh tokens, and client authorization credentials generated during authorization are stored strictly on the owner's local, encrypted file system. Credentials never leave the local operating environment.

We uphold a strict Zero Third-Party Sharing Guarantee:

  • No External Transmission: Tokens and channel data are never transmitted to external cloud servers, third-party analytics platforms, or intermediary proxy servers.
  • No Commercial Exploitation: We will NEVER sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your Google account credentials, tokens, or uploaded content metadata.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: No advertising networks or tracking software are embedded within our automation engine.
  • No AI Model Training on User Data: Your authentication data and channel credentials are not used to train machine learning models.

5 Revocation of Access & Data Deletion

You maintain continuous, unilateral control over your Google and YouTube account permissions at all times.

How to Revoke Access via Google Security:
You can inspect, manage, or instantly revoke DarkLore Automation’s access to your Google Account at any time by visiting the official Google Security Permissions console:

👉 https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Local Data Removal:
If access is revoked or if you wish to wipe local session tokens, simply delete the local authorization token file (token.json or equivalent local keystore) on your workstation. Once deleted or revoked, DarkLore Automation loses all capability to communicate with YouTube API Services on your behalf.

6 Data Retention Policy

DarkLore Automation only maintains temporary session cache files locally for the duration of the video batch generation and upload process. No historical subscriber logs or personal records are kept. Video files and rendered assets are retained locally solely based on the user's manual storage configuration.

7 Children's Privacy Protection

DarkLore Automation and UdemyTAG Studio do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13. Content uploaded via the tool is flagged with appropriate COPPA child-directed audience flags (madeForKids = false) during YouTube upload metadata creation.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in the DarkLore Automation pipeline, changes in Google Cloud OAuth guidelines, or amendments to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
